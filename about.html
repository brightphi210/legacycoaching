ABOUT LEGACY IMPACT COACHING ACADEMY

Building people who build better futures.

LICA was established from a simple but profound conviction: people can achieve significantly more when they have the right understanding, environment, strategy, systems, accountability and support.

LICA is designed to serve people at different stages of life and leadership — from students discovering their purpose and preparing for life, to parents intentionally raising the next generation, to entrepreneurs building businesses, professionals pursuing excellence, executives leading organisations and institutions seeking sustainable human performance.

We believe transformation must go beyond information.

  • Information tells you what to know.
  • Education helps you understand.
  • Coaching helps you discover.
  • Strategy helps you decide.
  • Systems help you execute.
  • Accountability helps you persist.

Transformation occurs when all these elements work together in seamless harmony.

OUR VISION

To become a globally respected transformation institution that develops exceptional people, high-performing families, purposeful leaders, thriving entrepreneurs and impact-driven organisations capable of creating enduring legacies.

OUR MISSION

To equip individuals, families and organisations with the knowledge, mindset, strategies, systems, skills, character and support required to unlock potential, achieve exceptional performance and create meaningful, measurable and lasting impact.

FOUNDER & PRINCIPAL TRANSFORMATION PARTNER

Miebi Francis Ofubu

Disruption Strategist · Polymath · Thought Leader · Coach · Educator · Author · Leadership & Performance Strategist · Legacy Parenting & Family Specialist

Miebi Francis Ofubu is the Founder and Principal Transformation Partner of Legacy Impact Coaching Academy Ltd., a multidisciplinary transformation institution committed to helping people and organisations unlock potential, achieve exceptional performance and create lasting impact.

With more than two decades of experience across coaching, education, leadership, business development, sales, mentoring and human transformation, he has coached, trained and mentored thousands of students, entrepreneurs, professionals and leaders seeking meaningful growth.

He is particularly passionate about helping people move from potential to performance, from confusion to clarity, from intention to execution, from success to significance, and from achievement to legacy.

"Success is not simply about what you achieve. It is about who you become, what you build, whom you impact and what continues because you lived."

PROFESSIONAL BACKGROUND

A multidisciplinary mind.

Miebi's work reflects a broad range of interests spanning coaching, leadership, parenting, family transformation, education, business, entrepreneurship, sales and organisational development — the multidisciplinary orientation behind his identity as a polymath and disruption strategist.

Miebi is a graduate of Rivers State University of Science and Technology, where he graduated as the Best Graduating Student in the Department of Agriculture & Environmental Engineering in 2002.

He is a Certified Management Consultant, Fellow of the Institute of Management Consultants, Fellow of the Institute of Professional Entrepreneurs & Strategic Administrators of Nigeria, Master Practitioner of NLP, Certified Positive Parenting Coach, Certified Life Coach, Certified Special Educational Needs Educator and International Curriculum Advisor. He has also undertaken leadership and management development with the Luxury Academy, London.

His work spans parenting & family transformation, student development, business & entrepreneurship, leadership, performance transformation and education.
